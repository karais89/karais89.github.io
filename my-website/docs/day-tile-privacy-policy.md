# Day Tile Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-31

Day Tile is a small monthly diary app for Android. This privacy policy explains how Day Tile handles information.

## Information We Collect

Day Tile does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, analytics events, or other personal information.

When you use the app, the diary text you enter, the selected date, and the selected color are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. Day Tile does not send this information to a server and does not share it with third parties.

## Storage and Deletion

Your diary entries are stored locally on your Android device. You can delete individual entries inside the app. If you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in Android settings, the locally saved entries are deleted from that device.

## Permissions

The release version of Day Tile does not request dangerous Android permissions.

## Third-Party Services

Day Tile does not include advertising, analytics, cloud sync, login, or social sharing services.

If you install Day Tile through Google Play, Google may process information related to your Google Play account, download, device, or store activity under Google's own privacy policy.

## Children's Privacy

Day Tile is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when Day Tile changes how it handles information. Updates will be posted on this page.
